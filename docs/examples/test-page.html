What is CMP?

CMP (Consent Management Platform) helps websites comply with GDPR and other privacy regulations by giving users control over their personal data.

IAB TCF

IAB Europe Transparency & Consent Framework (TCF) is an industry standard that enables websites, advertisers, and technology providers to process user data in compliance with regulations.

How does consent work?

When you visit a site for the first time, a consent banner is displayed. You can:

  • Accept all consents
  • Go to advanced settings and choose specific data processing purposes
  • Reject all optional consents

Your preferences are saved in the "eupubconsent-v2" cookie as a TC String containing encoded information about your consent choices.
